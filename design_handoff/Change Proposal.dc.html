Fix: importing from Google Tasks overwrites your local edits

Replace the one-way mirror with a 3-way merge plus opt-in write-back. Non-conflicting edits reconcile automatically in both directions; genuine conflicts surface a resolution modal instead of silently clobbering your edit.

＋ NEW A local edit survives a re-import
GIVENa task imported from Google, then renamed locally in Steady.
WHENthe same Google list is imported a second time.
THENthe conflict modal appears showing both titles — the local edit is never silently overwritten.
WHY This is the exact bug being fixed. The old one-way mirror clobbered local edits with no warning — this test is the regression guard that proves it can't happen again.
＋ NEW A resolution sticks after reload
GIVENan open conflict modal where the user picks the Steady value and confirms.
WHENthe app is reloaded and the list re-imported.
THENthe chosen value remains and the conflict does not reappear.
WHY Proves the merge base actually advanced server-side. Without it a "resolution" could be UI-only state that silently re-conflicts on the next sync.
